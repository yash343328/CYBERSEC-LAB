🔑 Caesar Cipher

Caesar Cipher ek sabse purana aur simple encryption technique hai. Har letter ko alphabet mein ek fixed number ke barabar shift kiya jaata hai. For example, shift 3 ke saath "A" → "D", "B" → "E". Julius Caesar ne apne secret messages ke liye iska use kiya tha.

How it works: Agar aapka plaintext hai "HELLO" aur shift hai 3, toh ciphertext hoga "KHOOR".
H(7)+3=K(10), E(4)+3=H(7), L(11)+3=O(14), L(11)+3=O(14), O(14)+3=R(17)
Weakness: Sirf 25 possible shifts hain — brute force se easily crack ho jaata hai!

  • Substitution cipher ka basic concept

  • Caesar cipher insecure kyun hai (small keyspace)
